**Full description** (4000):

> What will your family remember you for?
>
> Heirloom is a life simulator that doesn't end when you do. Be born, grow up, fall in love, chase a career, make bad decisions and good ones. When your life ends, continue as your child, and inherit the house, the money, the family business and the heirlooms that make your family yours.
>
> A LIFE IN YEARS
> Tap Age Up and watch a life unfold, one year at a time. Hundreds of moments ask you to choose: call out the manager who stole your work, sneak out to the party, adopt the white kitten on the doormat, sell grandpa's watch to pay rent… or don't.
>
> HEIRLOOMS WITH STORIES
> Grandpa's pocket watch can rewind one moment per life. Grandma's ring makes your wedding sweeter. Buy a piano for your kids, paint a picture, win a trophy, and watch them pass from generation to generation, each with its own history.
>
> YOUR FAMILY, YOUR DYNASTY
> Talk to your parents, spoil your kids, argue with your siblings. Write a will. Watch your family tree grow and your legacy score climb.
>
> A HOME TO MAKE YOURS
> Decorate your room, buy your first flat, and leave it to the next generation.
>
> RIBBONS AND LIFE GOALS
> Every life gets three goals and ends with a ribbon: Soulmates, Black Sheep, Centenarian, Tycoon, Superstar… Collect them all.
>
> EXPANSIONS
> • Fame: form a band, write songs, release albums, go on tour from 5 cities to 50, go viral, survive scandals.
> • Boss: start a café, a tea brand, a tech startup or a game studio. Raise money, IPO, or go bust. Pass the family business down.
> • Türkiye: the university exam, military service, henna nights, bayram visits and the neighbor auntie.
> • Campus: pick a major, chase a 4.0, join clubs, land internships, study abroad, go all the way to a PhD.
> • Lineage: a crest, a family seat, a rule for who comes next, an old feud, and a letter to those who follow.
> • Property: a flat to let, a few shares, a plot of land and the choices around money that sits still.
> • Care: look after the people who looked after you, and meet the slower years of your own body.
> • Crime World (18+): street hustles, heists, trials, prison, and the long road back.
>
> Play in English or Turkish.
>
> THEMES
> Switch the whole look of the game, from the warm Evening Album to the gold-on-black Coal & Gold.
>
> No account. No internet needed. Ads only if you choose to watch one.
